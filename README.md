# Mogbot

Your own team of AI agents, on your Mac, on the Claude or ChatGPT plan you
already pay for.

You talk to a Chief of Staff. It creates agents for the work you hand it,
briefs them, delegates and comes back with results. Each agent runs on
Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode on your Mac, signed in to your own account.
Anything that spends money, sends something as you or changes what other
people see waits for your Allow.

## Download

**[Mogbot 1.0.1 for Mac (Apple Silicon)](https://github.com/native-io-team/mogbot-releases/releases/download/v1.0.1/Mogbot_1.0.1_aarch64.dmg)**

Needs macOS 14 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac, and an account for Claude
Code, Codex or OpenCode. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

## Phone app

- iPhone: [TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/fsm64m5E)
- Android: Google Play, coming soon

The phone app is a remote for the Mogbot on your Mac. It pairs with a QR code
from Settings → Phone and talks to your Mac over your own Tailscale. Nothing
goes through our servers.

## Privacy

No account of ours, no analytics, no crash reports. Your agents, chats and
logins stay on your Mac. [Privacy policy](https://mogbot.app/privacy)
